About Us

Built on trust.
Driven by passion.

AutoFusion is a Kenyan car dealership founded on a simple idea: buying a car should be exciting, not exhausting. We source well-maintained locally used vehicles and direct imports — and every single one passes a strict multi-point inspection before it reaches our yard.

Whether you're after your first hatchback, a family SUV, or something with a bit more presence, our team walks with you from first enquiry to keys-in-hand.

  • Every vehicle fully inspected
  • Honest, transparent pricing — no hidden costs
  • Import & trade-in assistance
  • We handle the paperwork for you

Fair, Clear Pricing
